What We Do

Tinting Services Built Around You

From a single sedan to an entire office building, we match the right film to the job. Every install is measured, computer-cut and finished by hand — no bubbles, no peeling edges, no shortcuts.

01 · Automotive

Automotive Tinting

Your daily driver spends hours baking in parking lots and fighting low sun on the highway. The right tint transforms it — a cooler cabin, a sharper look, and an interior that stops fading and cracking.

We tint cars, trucks and SUVs of every make and model. Film is computer-cut to your exact glass pattern before it ever touches your vehicle, so there are no blades near your paint or seals and every edge lands factory-perfect.

  • Side & rear windows, windshield brow strips, and full packages
  • Shade options from nearly-clear 70% to full limo 5% — we help you pick a look that's also road-legal in BC
  • Old, purple or bubbled tint removed and replaced cleanly
  • Heat-shrunk, squeegeed and inspected edge by edge before pickup
  • Most vehicles done same-day — drop off in the morning, drive out that evening

02 · Residential

Residential Window Film

Big beautiful windows are the best part of your home — until summer afternoons turn the living room into a greenhouse and the sun bleaches your hardwood, couch and curtains.

Modern home films fix that without blacking out your view. Most of our residential installs are barely visible from inside — you keep the light and the scenery, and lose the heat, glare and UV.

  • Free in-home assessment — we measure and demo film samples on your actual glass
  • Heat-control films that can noticeably drop room temperatures on hot days
  • Daytime privacy films — see out, without the neighbours seeing in
  • 99% UV rejection to protect floors, furniture and artwork from fading
  • Clean, careful installs — we work around your furniture and leave no mess
Book a Free Home Assessment
03 · Commercial

Commercial Tinting

Glass-heavy buildings look great and cost a fortune to cool. Window film is one of the fastest payback upgrades a building can get — it cuts solar heat at the glass, evens out hot spots, and takes load off your HVAC.

We tint offices, storefronts, restaurants, clinics and warehouses across the Fraser Valley, and we schedule around your business hours so you never have to close.

  • Solar-control films that reduce cooling costs and tenant complaints
  • A clean, uniform exterior look across every pane
  • Glare control for offices full of screens
  • Privacy and frosted films for meeting rooms and street-facing glass
  • Evening and weekend installs available — zero business disruption
Request a Building Assessment
04 · RV & Fleet

RV & Fleet Tinting

Motorhomes and work vehicles live outside all day, every day. Tinting keeps RV interiors livable in July, protects cabinetry and upholstery from UV, and keeps tools and cargo in work vans out of sight.

For fleets, consistency is everything — we film every vehicle to the same spec so your company trucks look sharp and uniform, and we batch the work so your fleet never sits idle.

  • Motorhomes, travel trailers and campers — living-area and cab glass
  • Work vans, pickups and service vehicles tinted to one consistent spec
  • Security film options that keep smash-and-grab eyes off your equipment
  • Batch scheduling — we work through your fleet without downtime
  • One point of contact and simple invoicing for business accounts
