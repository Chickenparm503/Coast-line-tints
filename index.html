I · The Craft

Four disciplines. One standard.

Every installation — from a coupe to a storefront — receives the same measured, unhurried attention.

01

Automotive

Nano-ceramic film, computer-cut to your exact glass — never trimmed against it. Rejects heat, blocks 99% of UV, and settles into a deep factory finish that flatters the car rather than shouting over it. We advise on British Columbia tint regulations for every vehicle before a single panel is shaded.

  • Ceramic heat rejection
  • Computer-cut patterns
  • BC regulation guidance
02

Residential

Architectural film for the rooms you live in. Summer heat and glare are tempered, hardwood and furnishings are shielded from UV fade, and daytime privacy arrives without surrendering natural light or the view you paid for.

  • Heat & glare control
  • UV fade protection
  • Daytime privacy
03

Commercial

Storefronts, offices, and glass curtain walls. Solar films lower cooling costs and keep tenants comfortable; security and safety films hold glass together when it matters; frosted and branded privacy films give meeting rooms discretion with polish.

  • Energy savings
  • Security & safety film
  • Branded frosted privacy
04

RV & Fleet

Motorhomes, trailers, work trucks, and multi-vehicle fleets — finished to a single, consistent shade across every unit. Cooler cabs for your drivers, a sharper presence for your brand, and volume scheduling that works around your operation, not ours.

  • Consistent fleet shading
  • Driver cab comfort
  • Volume scheduling

III · The Standard

Measured. Not rushed.

I

Ceramic, as a rule

We fit nano-ceramic film as our standard, not an upsell — superior heat rejection with no signal interference and no fade to purple.

II

Cut by computer, placed by hand

Every pattern is plotted digitally to your exact glass, then finished by hand. No blades ever touch your vehicle or your windows.

III

Warranted for life

Our films carry a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, and discolouration — honoured here in Abbotsford, where we did the work.
