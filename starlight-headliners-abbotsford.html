What Is a Starlight Headliner?

A starlight headliner replaces or rebuilds your vehicle's headliner with hundreds to thousands of individual fiber-optic strands routed through the fabric, each one lit from a central LED source. The result is a ceiling of pinpoint “stars” inside your car — the same effect luxury brands like Rolls-Royce built their reputation on, done custom for your vehicle at a fraction of the cost.

At Coastline, this isn't a fiber kit stapled into your existing headliner. We do a full headliner rebuild — re-wrapping the board in suede or Alcantara and running the fiber-optic work into that new material — so the finished ceiling looks factory, not aftermarket.

Fiber Count Options

More fibers means a denser, more dramatic starfield. We don't go below 300 fibers — anything sparser than that looks thin rather than intentional. Choose based on how full you want the sky to look:

Not sure what's right for your vehicle? Send us a photo of your interior and we'll recommend a count.

Patterns & Customization

Beyond a plain starfield, we build:

  • Custom decal patterns — logos, initials, constellations, or a design you bring us, worked into the fiber layout
  • Shooting star effects — animated fiber runs that streak across the headliner rather than sitting static
  • Sunroof starlight — the same fiber-optic treatment extended around or integrated with a sunroof opening, for vehicles that have one
  • Custom color and color-changing (RGB) fiber options, paired with ambient lighting elsewhere in the cabin if you want the whole interior tied together

Installation Process & Timeline

  1. Consultation — we look at your vehicle, headliner condition, and what you want (fiber count, pattern, color)
  2. Headliner removal — your factory headliner comes out and the board is prepped
  3. Rebuild — the board is re-wrapped in suede or Alcantara, and fiber-optic strands are routed through the material in your chosen pattern
  4. Reinstall & wire-in — the finished headliner goes back in and is wired to its light source
  5. Quality check — every fiber is checked lit before the vehicle leaves

Turnaround is 2 days for most vehicles.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a starlight headliner installation take?

Most vehicles take 2 days from drop-off to a fully wired, checked install.

How many fibers do I need?

We won't go below 300 — that's our minimum for a look that reads as intentional rather than sparse. Most customers land in the 650–1500 range for a full, even sky; go higher for a denser, show-car look.

Can I get a custom pattern or logo?

Yes — we build custom decal patterns, shooting star effects, and can extend the treatment around a sunroof. Bring us a reference or idea and we'll tell you what's possible.

Is my factory headliner reused?

No — we rebuild the headliner board with new suede or Alcantara and route the fiber-optic work into that new material, so the finish looks factory rather than retrofitted.

What's the warranty?

Lifetime, on every starlight headliner install we do.

How much does it cost?

Starting around $400 for the 300-fiber option. Final price depends on fiber count and pattern — we'll quote you after a quick look at your vehicle and what you want.

Do you serve my area?

We're based in Abbotsford and install starlight headliners for customers throughout the Fraser Valley — Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, and Abbotsford itself.
