Ceramic Window Tint

Ceramic Window Tint — Abbotsford & the Fraser Valley

Nano-ceramic is the highest-performing window film we install — the coolest cabin, the clearest visibility, and the longest-lasting finish. If you want the best tint has to offer, this is it.

What Makes Ceramic Film Different?

Nano-ceramic particles reject the most heat of any film — even in nearly-clear shades — with the best optical clarity for night driving. There's no metal in the film, so there's zero interference with GPS, Bluetooth, cell signal, tire-pressure sensors or keyless entry. If you want the coolest cabin and the longest-lasting film, ceramic is our pick.

How Ceramic Compares

The shade is only half the story — what the film is made of decides how it performs and how long it lasts.

Dyed Film

The budget option

Darkens the glass for privacy and style at the lowest price point. Modest heat rejection, and cheap dyed films are the ones that turn purple and bubble after a few summers.

Carbon Film

The solid all-rounder

Blocks infrared heat much more effectively than dye, with a rich matte-black finish that never fades purple. Signal-safe, strong heat rejection at a fair price.

Ceramic Film

Maximum performance

Top-tier heat rejection even when light, crystal-clear visibility day and night, and the most durable film we install.

A quick word on BC tint laws

British Columbia restricts how dark the windshield and front side windows can be — rear side windows and the back window are far more flexible. Tell us your vehicle and the look you want, and we'll set you up with a package that looks right and passes inspection.

How We Install It

Film is computer-cut to your exact glass pattern before it ever touches your vehicle, so there are no blades near your paint or seals and every edge lands factory-perfect. It's heat-shrunk, squeegeed and inspected edge by edge before pickup — most vehicles are done same-day.

Ceramic tint pricing depends on your vehicle and how much glass you're covering. We quote after a quick look at your vehicle rather than posting a flat price list — call or send us your details and we'll get you a number fast.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a ceramic tint install take?

Most cars take 2–4 hours for a full package. Single windows or brow strips can be under an hour.

When can I roll my windows down after tinting?

Give it 3–5 days. The film needs time to cure against the glass, and rolling the window early can shift the edge before it's bonded.

Why does my tint look hazy or have little water pockets at first?

Totally normal. That's the mounting solution evaporating through the film as it cures. It clears on its own within days and the film dries perfectly flat.

Will ceramic tint interfere with my phone, GPS or radio?

No — ceramic film is metal-free, so there's no interference with GPS, Bluetooth, cell signal, tire-pressure sensors or keyless entry.

Can you remove my old purple, bubbled tint?

Yes — removal and replacement is one of our most common jobs. We strip the old film and adhesive cleanly and install fresh ceramic film the same visit.

What warranty do I get?

Our films carry manufacturer-backed warranties against bubbling, peeling, cracking and purple fading — and we stand behind our workmanship on every install.
