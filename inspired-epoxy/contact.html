Quick FAQ

Before You Ask

Do I need to empty the garage before you come?

Yes — the floor needs to be clear before grinding. If that's a problem, mention it in your quote request and we'll figure something out.

Can you quote from photos?

Usually, yes. Square footage plus a few photos of the concrete's condition gets you an accurate number for most residential jobs.

How far do you travel?

We cover the Lower Mainland. Outside that? Ask anyway — for larger projects we'll often make the trip.
