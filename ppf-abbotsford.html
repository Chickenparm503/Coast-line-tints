Paint Protection Film

Paint Protection Film (PPF) — Abbotsford & the Fraser Valley

Rock chips, road debris and swirl marks are the enemy of fresh paint. PPF is a virtually invisible layer that absorbs the hit instead of your clear coat — self-healing, and removable without a trace years down the road.

Get a Quote

What Does PPF Protect Against?

Paint protection film is a clear, self-healing urethane layer applied over your factory paint. It absorbs rock chips, road debris and swirl marks that would otherwise hit your clear coat directly — minor scratches in the film itself shrug off with a bit of heat, so the surface stays looking fresh.

Coverage Options

PPF can go anywhere from high-impact areas — the front bumper, hood, mirrors and rocker panels — up to a full-vehicle wrap for complete coverage. We'll walk through your vehicle and driving habits to recommend the coverage that makes sense for you.

Why PPF Over Nothing?

  • Self-healing film that shrugs off minor scratches and swirls
  • Absorbs rock chips and road debris instead of your clear coat
  • Computer-plotted panels for a seamless, factory-tight fit
  • 100% removable without damaging factory paint underneath

PPF pricing depends on your vehicle and how much coverage you want — from a few high-impact panels up to a full wrap. We quote after a quick look at your vehicle rather than posting a flat price list.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does PPF actually protect against?

Rock chips, road debris, minor scratches and swirl marks — the everyday wear that dulls fresh paint over time.

Can PPF be removed?

Yes — it's 100% removable without damaging the factory paint underneath.

Does PPF need special care?

It's wash-safe once cured. We'll walk you through the specific care recommendations for your film during your consultation.

Can I get partial coverage instead of the whole car?

Yes — most customers start with high-impact areas like the front bumper, hood and mirrors, and can add more coverage later.

Do you serve my area?

We're based in Abbotsford and install PPF for customers throughout the Fraser Valley — Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, and Abbotsford itself.
