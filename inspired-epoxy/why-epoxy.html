The truth about “1-day epoxy” kits and paint

Most failed floors fail at the bond, not the coating — acid-etched or unprepped concrete lets film peel under hot tires. That’s why we diamond-grind every slab, repair damage and moisture-check before a drop of epoxy goes down. Prep is the warranty.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does an epoxy floor take to install?

Most residential garages take one to two days depending on the system and the condition of the concrete. You can usually walk on the floor the next day and park on it within a few days of the final coat.

Will it peel like the paint I tried before?

No — DIY kits fail because they're thin coatings over poorly prepped concrete. We mechanically grind the slab so the 100% solids epoxy locks into the surface, then protect it with a UV-stable top coat.

Is epoxy slippery when wet?

Flake floors have a naturally slip-resistant texture, and we can add anti-slip aggregate to any smooth or high-gloss system — common for exterior steps, shops and commercial kitchens.

Can you fix cracked or pitted concrete first?

Yes. Crack repair, pit and spall patching are part of our prep on every job. Badly damaged slabs may need extra repair work, which we'll flag in your quote — never as a surprise.

How do I clean and maintain it?

Sweep or dust-mop regularly and wash occasionally with a mild cleaner. No waxing, no sealing, no special products — that's most of the point.

What does it cost?

It depends on square footage, concrete condition and the system you choose. Quotes are free, fast and honest — send us your details and we'll get you a number, usually the same day.
