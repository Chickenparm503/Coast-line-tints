We don't say that to talk you out of it; most of our Langley customers tell us the same thing, that the trip is easy and the results are worth it, especially when they're getting more than just tint done in one visit.

What We Tint

Cars, trucks and SUVs of every make and model, computer-cut to your exact glass pattern so every edge lands factory-perfect. Choose from three film types depending on your budget and priorities:

  • Dyed film — the most affordable way to get the look
  • Carbon film — strong heat rejection, matte-black finish that won't fade purple
  • Ceramic film — our top-performing option, the coolest cabin and clearest visibility — see our ceramic tint page for details

Since Langley customers are already making the drive, a lot combine tint with paint protection film, a wrap, a starlight headliner, or ambient lighting in the same visit.

A quick word on BC tint laws

British Columbia restricts how dark the windshield and front side windows can be — rear side windows and the back window are far more flexible. Tell us your vehicle and the look you want, and we'll set you up with a package that looks right and passes inspection.
