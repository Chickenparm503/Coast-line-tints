Our Process

Floors That Last Start With Prep

Coatings fail when prep is skipped. We never skip it. Here's how we do it.

01

Diamond Grind & Repair

We mechanically grind the slab to open the pores, then fix cracks, pits and spalls — so the coating bonds to the concrete permanently.

02

Base Coat

A 100% solids epoxy base is rolled on and worked into the profile — the structural layer that gives the system its strength.

03

Broadcast & Top Coat

Flake or metallic effects go down, then a UV-stable clear top coat locks it all in. Walk on it the next day, park on it within days.
