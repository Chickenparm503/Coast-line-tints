What Is Ambient Lighting?

Ambient lighting is a custom-fit LED system built into your interior trim — along the dash, door cards, footwells and center console — so the glow comes from inside the panel lines instead of a strip stuck on top of them. It pairs perfectly with a tint or wrap package for a full interior-and-exterior refresh, but it's just as popular on its own.

What's Included

  • 16 million colors with app and voice control
  • Music-sync and multi-zone lighting modes
  • Clean, hidden wiring tucked behind factory panels
  • Fits factory trim with no permanent modification
  • Pairs great with tint, PPF or a wrap for a full refresh

Installation Process

  1. Consultation — we look at your interior and talk through zones, colors and control preferences
  2. Panel removal — trim panels come off to give clean access behind the dash, doors and console
  3. Routing — LED runs are routed and secured behind the trim, out of sight
  4. Controller wire-in — the control module is wired in and paired to the app or remote
  5. Testing & reassembly — every zone is tested before panels go back on

Turnaround depends on your vehicle and how many zones you're lighting — we'll give you a time estimate at your consultation.

Ambient lighting pricing depends on zone count and vehicle. We quote after a quick consultation rather than posting a flat price list — get in touch and we'll get you a number.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will ambient lighting drain my battery?

No — our kits are LED-based, which draw very little power. They're not going to strain your vehicle's electrical system.

Can I control the colors and brightness?

Yes — app and voice control is standard, with 16 million colors, music-sync and multi-zone modes so you can set different colors per area of the cabin.

Will the wiring be visible?

No — every run is routed and secured behind your factory trim panels, so all you see is the light, not the wiring.

Can this be combined with tint, PPF or a wrap?

Definitely — ambient lighting pairs well with any of our other services for a full interior-and-exterior refresh, or works great completely on its own.

Does it permanently modify my interior?

No — the kit fits your factory trim without permanent modification to the panels themselves.

Do you serve my area?

We're based in Abbotsford and install ambient lighting for customers throughout the Fraser Valley — Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, and Abbotsford itself.
