One Shop, the Whole Valley

Our shop is in Abbotsford, and customers regularly drive in from across the Fraser Valley — Chilliwack, Mission, Langley and everywhere in between. Whatever you're looking to do to your vehicle, it's one stop rather than bouncing between different shops for different jobs.

Everything We Do

  • Ceramic & standard window tint — automotive, residential and commercial
  • Paint protection film (PPF) — self-healing protection against chips and road debris
  • Vehicle wraps — full color-change, matte, satin, gloss and chrome-delete finishes
  • Starlight headliners — custom fiber-optic ceilings, 300–3000 fibers
  • Ambient interior lighting — app-controlled RGB lighting throughout the cabin

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have more than one location?

No — one shop, in Abbotsford. We serve the whole Fraser Valley from there, and most customers find the drive in worth it.

Can I get multiple services done in one visit?

Yes — a lot of customers combine tint with PPF, a wrap, a starlight headliner or ambient lighting on the same visit, especially if they're driving in from further out.

How do I get a quote?

Call, text, or send us your details through our contact page — we usually reply the same day.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 7am to 11pm. Closed Sundays.

Ready to build your vehicle?
