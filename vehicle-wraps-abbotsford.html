What Is a Vehicle Wrap?

A vehicle wrap is a vinyl film applied over your factory paint to change its color or finish — a full color change, a satin or matte look, chrome-delete trim, or accent panels. It's a cheaper, fully reversible alternative to a repaint, and it protects the factory paint underneath while it's on.

Finish Options

  • Full color-change wraps or partial/accent wraps
  • Matte, satin, gloss and chrome-delete finishes available
  • Computer-plotted panels for a seamless, bubble-free finish
  • Removable without damaging the factory paint underneath

Wrap or PPF?

Wraps are about changing your look. PPF is about protecting the look you already have. They're not mutually exclusive — plenty of our customers wrap a car and protect the high-impact panels with PPF underneath. Tell us what you're going for and we'll recommend the right combination.

Wrap pricing depends on your vehicle, the finish you choose, and how much coverage you want. We quote after a quick look at your vehicle rather than posting a flat price list.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a wrap better than a repaint?

It depends on your goals — a wrap is cheaper, fully reversible, and protects the paint underneath while it's on. A repaint is permanent. Most customers choose a wrap for exactly those reasons.

Can you wrap over existing paint damage?

Wraps look best over sound, properly prepped paint. If there's damage, we'll flag it during your consultation so there are no surprises.

Can I get a partial wrap instead of the whole car?

Yes — accent panels and partial wraps are common, whether for style or budget reasons.

Can a wrap be removed later?

Yes — wraps are removable without damaging the factory paint underneath.

Do you serve my area?

We're based in Abbotsford and install vehicle wraps for customers throughout the Fraser Valley — Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, and Abbotsford itself.
